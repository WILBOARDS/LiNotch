# 🕳️ Linux Notch Bar (Dynamic Island Clone) - MVP & Workflow

## 🎯 Concept
A centered, rounded "notch" at the top of your screen that:
- **Collapsed**: Shows time, battery, status icons (pill shape, ~200x40px)
- **Expanded**: Drops down to show notifications, media controls, quick settings (~400x300px)
- **Interactions**: Click to toggle, hover to peek, auto-collapse after timeout
- **Aesthetic**: Black glassmorphism with backdrop blur, smooth animations
